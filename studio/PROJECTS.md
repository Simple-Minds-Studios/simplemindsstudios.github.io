# Simple Minds Studios Public Project Index

[Browse the project directory](https://simplemindsstudios.com/projects/). Status labels describe the current public presentation, not a guarantee of production readiness. Public links checked November 20, 2026.

## Family History & Memory

- **The Anonymous Family** — Live exhibit. A privacy-first family-history exhibit exploring migration, memory, evidence, and archival uncertainty. Project steward: Brad Devowe. [Explore](https://simple-minds-studios.github.io/the-anonymous-family/). [Repository](https://github.com/Simple-Minds-Studios/the-anonymous-family).
- **STILL HERE — A Tree of Memory** — Live exhibit. A family-history exhibition with distinct evidence labels and a public/private publication boundary. Project steward: Bart Salazar. [Explore](https://simple-minds-studios.github.io/still-here/). [Repository](https://github.com/Simple-Minds-Studios/still-here).

## Research & Exploration

- **LensBridge** — Research tools. Astronomy research connecting historical microlensing measurements, event identities, and source provenance ahead of Roman science. [Explore](https://simple-minds-studios.github.io/RomanMAST/). [Repository](https://github.com/Simple-Minds-Studios/RomanMAST).
- **Voyager III** — Research concept. An exploratory space-mission concept. View the current public presentation and development archive. [Explore](https://simple-minds-studios.github.io/project-voyager-iii/). [Repository](https://github.com/Simple-Minds-Studios/project-voyager-iii).
- **Project Wayfinder** — Research concept. An investigation presented as The Hidden Social Network of Neolithic Britain. [Explore](https://simple-minds-studios.github.io/project-wayfinder/). [Repository](https://github.com/Simple-Minds-Studios/project-wayfinder).
- **Project Aurelia** — Research concept. A developing research project with a public presentation and source archive. [Explore](https://simple-minds-studios.github.io/project-aurelia/). [Repository](https://github.com/Simple-Minds-Studios/project-aurelia).
- **WITNESS / MULTIPLE** — Research archive. A polyphonic archive of human–AI collaboration, preserving distinct interpretations, provenance, and disagreement. [Explore](https://simple-minds-studios.github.io/witness-multiple/). [Repository](https://github.com/Simple-Minds-Studios/witness-multiple).
- **The Moving Frame** — In development. A human–AI discoveries archive. Its public landing page is live; protocols and findings are in preparation. [Explore](https://simple-minds-studios.github.io/the-moving-frame/). [Repository](https://github.com/Simple-Minds-Studios/the-moving-frame).
